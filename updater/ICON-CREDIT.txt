Updater icon: NONE. rg-updater ships the standard macOS application icon by explicit opt-in
(build_updater.sh --generic-icon), so no artwork is embedded.

Deliberate: ripgrep has no logo of its own to carry, and this product is an unofficial community
build that must not read as official. A stand-in .icns would be exactly the placeholder the icon gate
rejects.

Exit: when an original Mavergreen ripgrep mark exists, add updater/rg.icns and pass it to
build_updater.sh as --icon in build/build-updater.sh, instead of --generic-icon.
